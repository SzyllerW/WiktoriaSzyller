Soror – Boss Rush Project

Soror is a boss-rush action game created as a passion project for my bachelor's thesis. Our team of four developers collaborated on its design, worldbuilding and gameplay systems.

My role focused entirely on enemy AI and combat programming, including:

  • designing the entire boss behaviour architecture (FSM-driven)
  • implementing melee collision & hit detection systems
  • creating AOE telegraph logic and multi-phase patterns
  • building a custom Unity Editor window for aligning AOE shapes to floors and uneven geometry

The project emphasizes gameplay feel, telegraph readability, and precise timing between the player and the boss.

Code Highlights

Sub-stepped melee collision: the sword’s motion is sampled several times per frame using capsule checks along the blade arc. This prevents tunneling and ensures consistent hit detection.
Adaptive rotation blending: fast anticipation turn-in followed by slower strike-window tracking. This makes attacks readable without losing threat or fluidity.
