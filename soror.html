Soror – Boss Rush Project

About the Project

Soror is a boss-rush action game created as a passion project for my bachelor's thesis. The game was developed by a 4-person team, where my role focused on gameplay programming:

  • designing and implementing enemy AI and boss combat behaviour
  • creating AOE patterns and telegraph systems
  • building a custom Unity Editor window to align AOE shapes to floors and level geometry

My work centered on combat feel, readable but challenging boss attacks, and systems that allow designers to iterate quickly on encounters.

Gameplay Video

Code Highlights

Sub-stepped melee collision: the sword’s motion is sampled multiple times per frame using capsule checks along the blade arc, preventing tunneling and ensuring accurate, high-speed hit detection.
Adaptive rotation blending: fast anticipation turn-in followed by slower tracking during the attack window. This makes boss attacks readable and well-telegraphed without losing threat or fluidity.
