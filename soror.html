Soror – Boss Rush Project

About the Project

Soror is a boss-rush passion project created as part of my bachelor's thesis. The game was developed by a 4-person team, where I served as the enemy AI and combat systems programmer.

I implemented the entire boss behaviour architecture, advanced melee collision detection, attack windows, posture/stagger logic, and the AOE telegraph system. Additionally, I built a custom Unity Editor window for placing and aligning AOE telegraphs to floors and level geometry, improving workflow and designer iteration speed.

Code Highlights

Sub-stepped melee collision: the sword’s motion is sampled multiple times per frame using capsule checks along the entire blade path. This prevents tunneling and makes hits accurate at any framerate.
Adaptive rotation blending: the boss rotates quickly in anticipation, then slows during the strike window, making attacks readable yet threatening and improving combat clarity.
